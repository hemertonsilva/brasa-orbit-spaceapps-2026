Brasa Orbit

A daily calendar of satellite fire detections, MODIS and VIIRS side by side, that shows where the two records agree, where they diverge, and what they cannot tell us.

Western Mato Grosso · 2018–2020

Detections per day

    Same scale for both sensors. A light gray cell means no detection recorded — not “no fire”: the files carry no cloud or coverage information.

    What these numbers are — and are not

    • Each count is a NASA FIRMS active-fire detection (a pixel flagged with one or more fires or thermal anomalies), filtered to type = 0 (presumed vegetation fire). It is not a count of fires and not burned area.
    • MODIS pixels are about 1 km; VIIRS I-band pixels are 375 m, and the satellites pass at different times. Raw counts from the two sensors are not directly comparable; this view does not harmonize them.
    • Dates are the acquisition date in UTC as published by FIRMS. Data: FIRMS standard (archive) yearly country files for Brazil — MODIS version 6.03 and VIIRS S-NPP version 2, as labelled in the files.
    • We acknowledge the use of imagery from the NASA LANCE FIRMS (earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms).
